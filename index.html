Overview and master plan

A Five-Acre Green at the Heart of Aravista

Aravista is planned across approximately 20 acres in New Gurugram. At its centre is a five-acre green, part of 9.5 acres of planned open space across the wider development. The landscape plan brings together a wellness garden, a nature trail and water features, with the Central Social Club and The Arena Sports Club giving residents dedicated space for leisure and sport.

The 3 and 4 BHK residences bring that outdoor space closer to home with sundecks. Ask for the master plan to see where your preferred tower sits in relation to the green, the clubs and the approach roads.
